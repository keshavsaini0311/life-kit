---
name: life-review
description: Review a Life Kit workspace and suggest realistic next actions from the user's context and recorded observations.
---

Read `AGENTS.md`, `personal/rules.md`, `life.json` and only relevant domain context.
Run `life-kit --workspace $workspace status` or review supplied files if the CLI is
unavailable. Explain coverage and stale dates. No records means unknown, not zero.
Study minutes are self-reported, not completed lessons or demonstrated improvement.
An outcome credits only its explicit activity. Don't add stage counts as people counts.

Give a short review of known facts, what needs refreshing and one or two realistic
next actions. Ask a material missing question when needed. Don't log inferred emotions
or invented facts. Record supplied changes only when requested and preserve dates.

Reports stay local. A review does not authorize messages, purchases or account changes.
Configuration is not authentication; check a verified read before relying on a source.
